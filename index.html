The Custom Grind Bar

The coffee aisle, rebuilt as a workshop.

No bags chosen for you. You pick the beans, you set the ratio, you choose the grind, and you name the blend.

  1. 1

    Pick your beans

    Twelve to fifteen single origins line the dispenser wall, light to dark, plus decaf. Start with a base you love or a bean you have never tried.

  2. 2

    Mix your ratio

    Scales and mixing bowls are on the station. A good starting point is 70 percent Colombian Supremo and 30 percent Ethiopian Yirgacheffe.

  3. 3

    Grind it your way

    Commercial retail grinders are set up with clear guides for French Press, Drip, and Espresso, so your grind matches your brewer.

  4. 4

    Name it and label it

    A tablet kiosk prints your label with your blend name, the roast date, flavor notes, and your bean ratio.

DIY Tasting Station

Bought beans? Brew a cup here.

The tasting station is a standing-room counter with a commercial hot water tower and shared brewing gear, pour-overs and French presses. Buy any beans and brew a cup on the spot to taste what you just built.

It is free with a bean purchase, and it is the best way to test a blend before you take it home. Shared gear is sanitized between uses.

Brewing gear

Take the ritual home.

Pour-over cones, filters, French presses, and airtight storage canisters. Everything you need to brew your blend the way it was ground for.
