# Coffee Happy

Public website for Coffee Happy, a hands-on coffee shop coming to Plymouth, Massachusetts.

## The idea

Customers build their own blend at the Custom Grind Bar: pick beans from a dispenser wall of 12 to 15 single origins, mix a ratio on the station scales, grind it fresh on commercial grinders, and name it at a tablet kiosk that prints a label with the roast date, flavor notes, and bean ratio. Pre-bagged house blends are on the shelf for quick stops, and a DIY Tasting Station lets anyone who buys beans brew a cup on site.

## What is on the site

- The Custom Grind Bar, how it works in four steps
- An interactive blend mixer: pick two beans, set the ratio, name the blend
- The seven house blends: Plymouth Rock-et Fuel, Not A Morning Person, 404 Sleep Not Found, Wicked Awake, The Apprentice Cup, Third Shift, and The Foreman
- The DIY Tasting Station and brewing gear
- Visit info for Plymouth, Massachusetts

## Run it locally

No build step and no backend. Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder:

```text
python3 -m http.server
```

then visit `http://localhost:8000`.
